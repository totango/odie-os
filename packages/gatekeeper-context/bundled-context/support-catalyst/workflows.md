# Workflow troubleshooting notes

For workflow issues, separate triggering, eligibility, delivery, and visibility.

- Triggering: did the event or schedule run?
- Eligibility: did filters include the intended accounts or users?
- Delivery: did the task, alert, or notification enqueue successfully?
- Visibility: does the requester have permission to see the result?

Prefer screenshots or sanitized field names over raw exported records.
